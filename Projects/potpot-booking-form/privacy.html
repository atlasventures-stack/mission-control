Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

PotPot ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Atlas Ventures, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our plant care services and website.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you book our services, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, WhatsApp number
  • Address Details: House/flat number, building name, full address, pincode, and location coordinates
  • Service Information: Number of plants, booking dates, service history

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • IP address and location data
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Referral source

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and manage your bookings
  • Provide plant care services at your location
  • Communicate with you about appointments and service updates
  • Send service reminders via WhatsApp
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Analyze website usage and optimize our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Our gardeners who provide services at your location (they receive only necessary information like name, address, and scheduled time)
  • Technology Partners: Services that help us operate our business (Google Maps for location services, WhatsApp/WATI for communications)
  • Analytics Services: Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, and Amplitude for website analytics, advertising, and service improvement. This may include personal information such as name and phone number to help us understand user journeys and improve our service.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses:

  • Google Analytics: To understand how visitors use our website
  • Meta Pixel: To measure the effectiveness of our advertising
  • Amplitude: To analyze user behavior, track booking journeys, and improve our service. This includes session replay and may associate your name and phone number with your activity to provide better support and service.
  • Essential Cookies: To enable basic website functionality

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Secure data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Limited access to personal data
  • Regular security reviews

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Service records may be retained for up to 3 years for business and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
